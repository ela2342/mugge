## US-161: Amplifier chain calibration (validate "Mult 1.0 = 450 W")

**As** the engineer protecting low-power drivers,
**I want** the gain chain calibrated from Mult to amplifier output,
**so that** the US-159 G9 per-speaker ceilings rest on a measured value instead of the worst-case assumption A35.

**Status:** draft (orphan from US-159 G9, filed by PM at PO request 2026-10-24). AE scoped the ACs 2026-10-24.
**Priority:** medium-high (PO recommendation; owner sets): after the US-159 core, before any CHN-50P gig
**Related:** US-159 G9, F-322, A35, D-009, D-029

### Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] AC1: Per channel 1-4: 1 kHz sine from signal-gen at -40 dBFS (cap -20 dBFS), amps at their marked gig knob positions, into a resistive dummy load of the rated impedance (never into speakers). Measure true-RMS volts at the amp output and, at the same time, the ADA8200 line output.
- [ ] AC2: Derive watts at Mult 1.0 / 0 dBFS (P = V^2/R, extrapolated in dB) and check the ADA8200 maximum output against the amp input sensitivity (where the amp clips). Record the result in the amp/hardware config. This validates or replaces A35 and recomputes the G9 ceilings: if the chain delivers less, ceilings may be relaxed only with AE review; if more, they tighten immediately.
- [ ] AC3: Amp gain knob positions are physically marked. The calibration is valid only at those positions; the pre-flight checklist gets "knobs at marks".
- [ ] AC4 (O-11): ch 5-8: ADA8200 output level into the IEM transmitter and headphone amp at -20 dBFS. Earpiece SPL with a coupler if the owner has one; otherwise an open item.
- [ ] AC5: Safety: amps on only in a CM CHANGE session with the owner present; D-063 gate opened per safety.md; signal-gen cap verified (pre-flight #6); IEMs disconnected. Needs owner hardware (dummy loads, true-RMS meter).

**Dependency:** pre-flight #10 (US-159 G11 not required).
