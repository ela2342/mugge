## US-161: Amplifier chain calibration (validate "Mult 1.0 = 450 W")

**As** the engineer protecting low-power drivers,
**I want** the gain chain calibrated from Mult to amplifier output,
**so that** the US-159 G9 per-speaker ceilings rest on a measured value instead of the worst-case assumption A35.

**Status:** draft (orphan from US-159 G9, filed by PM at PO request 2026-10-24). AE to scope.
**Related:** US-159 G9, F-322, A35, D-009, D-029

### Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] AC1: AE defines the measurement (amps on, dummy load or speaker, method and safety limits).
- [ ] AC2: Measured Mult-to-watts per amp channel recorded; A35 marked validated or corrected.
- [ ] AC3: G9 ceilings recomputed from the measurement if it differs.
