## US-164: Pi 5 RT and audio re-baseline

**As** the owner moving the rig from the Pi 4B to the Pi 5,
**I want** the RT and audio performance numbers re-measured on the Pi 5,
**so that** the quantum, filter-length and CPU budget decisions made on the Pi 4B are known to hold before any gig.

**Status:** draft (PO, 2026-10-24; architect's 163c, accepted by the architect)
**Priority:** high (PO: no Pi 5 gig use without it)
**Depends on:** US-163; hard prerequisite: an active cooler is fitted and the thermal behaviour characterised (PO, architect; O-39). It blocks all load and RT work on `.88` and any amps-on under load, not the US-163 no-audio bootstrap
**Related:** D-011, D-013, D-040 (BM-2), F-015, F-295 (#40's original: USB jitter on the Pi 4's VL805; the Pi 5 has RP1), F-331, F-353, F-358, US-165. AE: RT behaviour of mugge's build on Pi 5 hardware (PREEMPT_RT, FIFO 88, xruns, RP1 USB) is unverified until this story

Risk (QE, PO), also for every later load test (T18/T19, the BM-2
re-run under F-353): no active cooling on the Pi 5 as found;
architect and AE rule before any sustained audio load.

### Acceptance criteria

- [ ] AC1: RP1 USB isochronous behaviour, xrun rate and F-015 contention measured. Includes F-358 item 2: ERR/min at `period-num` 4 and 5, at q1024 and q256, and the sink delay at q256 for both (no extra Live-mode PA-path latency, AE).
- [ ] AC2: T1-T4 re-run on the Pi 5.
- [ ] AC3: BM-2 convolver CPU re-measured (16k taps, 8 ch).
- [ ] AC4: 30-minute stability run (T3d).
- [ ] AC5: Quantum 256 (Live) and 1024 (DJ) re-validated.
- [ ] AC6: Flight-case thermal behaviour measured.
- [ ] AC7: 16K vs 4K pages evaluated, measured, joint architect and AE (D-013 precondition).
