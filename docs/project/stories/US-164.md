## US-164: Pi 5 RT and audio re-baseline

**As** the owner moving the rig from the Pi 4B to the Pi 5,
**I want** the RT and audio performance numbers re-measured on the Pi 5,
**so that** the quantum, filter-length and CPU budget decisions made on the Pi 4B are known to hold before any gig.

**Status:** draft (PO, 2026-10-24; architect's 163c, accepted by the architect)
**Priority:** high (PO: no Pi 5 gig use without it)
**Depends on:** US-163
**Related:** D-011, D-013, D-040 (BM-2), F-015, F-331, F-353, US-165

### Acceptance criteria

- [ ] AC1: RP1 USB isochronous behaviour, xrun rate and F-015 contention measured.
- [ ] AC2: T1-T4 re-run on the Pi 5.
- [ ] AC3: BM-2 convolver CPU re-measured (16k taps, 8 ch).
- [ ] AC4: 30-minute stability run (T3d).
- [ ] AC5: Quantum 256 (Live) and 1024 (DJ) re-validated.
- [ ] AC6: Flight-case thermal behaviour measured.
- [ ] AC7: 16K vs 4K pages evaluated, measured, joint architect and AE (D-013 precondition).
