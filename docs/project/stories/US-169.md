## US-169: D-066 on main: per-identity sealed-sub HPF exemption, quantified and enforced

**As** the sound engineer,
**I want** the subsonic-HPF exemption granted only to speaker identities that are proven safe,
**so that** a small sealed sub can never run unprotected on a powerful channel.

**Status:** draft (PO, 2026-10-24, from AE's verdict on #40's D-066 text)
**Priority:** medium (PO, AE): no hazard today (Ultimax about 12-14 mm against 28 mm Xmax). It becomes high as soon as any other sealed identity is proposed, so it must land before any new sealed sub identity
**Depends on:** none
**Related:** D-031, D-066 (#40 only, `c1832030`; its text is defective per AE, do not cherry-pick it as-is), US-155, F-349, F-321
**Reviewers:** AE (veto), QE, architect

AE's landing conditions (PO): (a) a genuinely sealed (non-leaky) box of
about 100 L or more; ported or vented voids it; (b) the bound is the
amp's peak output voltage, stated in volts (about 70 V peak or less),
not "450 W"; bridged, higher-rail or parallel-DVC (1 ohm) wiring voids
it; (c) driver excursion protection only, not SPL, room modes or DC/NaN
events; (d) this identity only, not "sealed subs" in general.

Main's code treats "no HPF" inconsistently today (AE): `runner.py:216`
builds a 0 Hz HPF for `0`, which verify then fails (fail-safe but
broken); `speaker_routes.py:567` flags `null` as a D-031 violation;
`speaker_routes.py:218` rejects `null`.

### Acceptance criteria

- [ ] AC1: D-066 is recorded on main in amended form. The exemption is per identity, not per enclosure class. Criterion: worst-case peak excursion x_pk ≈ Bl·V_pk / (Re·(1+α)/Cms) × peaking(Qtc), with V_pk the amp's clipping peak voltage on that channel, and x_pk ≤ 0.7·Xmax.
- [ ] AC2: Scope conditions stated: a genuinely sealed (non-leaky) box of declared volume; ported, PR, bandpass and isobaric excluded unless analysed separately; any change of amp, bridging, DVC wiring or box voids the exemption; excursion only, with DC/NaN events (F-349) out of scope.
- [ ] AC3: Physics corrections to #40's text: Fc instead of Fs; "displacement-limited by air spring" restated as force/stiffness; the "crossover slope provides natural rolloff" claim deleted; "15mm+ Xmax" removed or turned into a criterion.
- [ ] AC4: One representation: `mandatory_hpf_hz: null` plus `hpf_exemption: {decision: D-066, amp, wiring, v_pk, x_pk, xmax}`. `0` is rejected explicitly, with a clear error.
- [ ] AC5: The validator (web UI `speaker_routes.py` profile check 5 and identity create, line 218), the runner (`room-correction/runner.py:216`) and `verify_mandatory_hpf` are aligned. A `null` without a passing `hpf_exemption` record is a D-031 violation; with the record the identity is accepted and the runner builds no HPF.
- [ ] AC6: The Ultimax identity on main gets an `hpf_exemption` record with AE's numbers (450 W / 4 ohm, about 60 V peak, about 70 V clip, x_pk about 12-14 mm, Xmax 28 mm). The comment in `dayton-ultimax-umii18-22-sealed-120l.yml` is restated in volts (replacing "450W cannot exceed"). The Ultimax profile then passes web UI validation; today it presumably fails, which is fail-safe.
- [ ] AC7: QE tests, each at the validator and the runner level: null with a valid record passes; null without a record fails; null with a record where x_pk > 0.7·Xmax fails; `0` is rejected; a small sealed identity (Xmax 8 mm, 450 W channel) fails.
