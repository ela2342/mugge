### Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] AC1: AE reviews the 200 Hz crossover and signs off on the frequency, the 24 dB/oct slope and the satellite/sub summation, using the CHN-50P sealed data (Fc 157 Hz, F3 107 Hz, 7 W thermal limit). AE also confirms the sub mono sum L+R and normal polarity.
- [ ] AC2: D-031 for the satellites. The 200 Hz HPF baked into the FIR (D-055) meets or exceeds `mandatory_hpf_hz: 80`. AE verifies this against the actual coefficient files, not just the YAML.
- [ ] AC3: D-031 exception for the sub. The identity sets `mandatory_hpf_hz: null` and calls it an "owner directive", but no decision record exists. Either the owner confirms it and a decision D-NNN (amending D-031 for sealed and Xmax >= 16 mm) is filed, or a subsonic HPF is added. Must be settled before acceptance.
- [ ] AC4: Coefficient correctness. The venue references the shared names `combined_left_hp.wav`, `combined_right_hp.wav` and `combined_sub1_lp.wav`. Risk: the 7 W CHN-50P could receive 80-200 Hz content.
- [ ] AC5: D-009 gain staging. AE confirms that mains -60 dB, sub -64 dB and spare -120 dB, together with profile `power_limit_db` (sat -22 dB, sub -6 dB) and headroom -7 dB, keep the CHN-50P under 7 W at full-scale input through the McGrey PA4504 (450 W @ 4 ohm). Arithmetic shown.

- [ ] AC7: Config validation passes (`nix run .#test-all` driver/venue/profile validation), and the venue appears and loads in the web UI venue selector in local-demo. Browser E2E or a documented manual check.

### Audio-engineer retroactive review (2026-10-24): conditional approve

- The config is sound. Two systemic defects show up through it, but this
  story didn't cause them: F-321 (venue coefficients are not enforced at
  runtime; this resolves AC4's risk) and F-322 (per-speaker
  thermal/excursion limits are not enforced; relevant to AC5).
- **AC3, sub D-031 exception, checked numerically: OK.** UMII18-22 in 120 L
  sealed: K_total ≈ 24.7 kN/m (Vas/Vb = 2.07), Fc ≈ 38.5 Hz, Qtc ≈ 0.93.
  At the PA4504's full output (450 W/4 Ω, ~60 V peak), excursion is
  ~11 mm (DC asymptote) and ~12 mm at Fc, against an Xmax of 28 mm. The
  exception holds for this amp. The owner still has to confirm it so a
  decision record can be filed (PO Q1).
- **Additional AC:** the identity file notes that the exception depends on
  amp output voltage and must be re-checked if the amp or bridging
  changes.
- AC1: 200 Hz LR4 for the CHN-50P (Fc 157 Hz) is a correct choice. Delays
  of 0 before measurement are fine. Ch 4 at -120 dB is fine.

- **AC5 cross-reference:** the CHN-50P static ceiling is <= -19 dB re amp
  full scale (Mult <= 0.112), worst case until chain calibration (F-322,
  US-159 G9).
