### H procedure (AE, 2026-10-25, oscilloscope)

Setup:
1. All speakers disconnected; measure the amp outputs unloaded (an
   unloaded Class D output reads equal or higher, so this errs safe).
   No HP/IEM.
2. Amp knobs ch 1-3 at minimum before power-on; ch 4 at minimum and
   left there. Per-channel filter switches flat; if there are none,
   use 50 Hz instead of 1 kHz for ch 3.
3. Test tone: 1 kHz sine at -20 dBFS peak, straight to the
   USBStreamer playback port of the channel measured, bypassing the
   convolver (worker and CM pick the route in a CHANGE session).
   Bypassing the gate is acceptable for this step ONLY because no
   speaker is connected. Speakers go back on only after the route is
   removed and the gate is closed with the 16 gain values read per
   safety.md "Gains at boot" (`pw-cli enum-params`, node states logged
   before and after; a `pw-dump` value is never a pass, F-395).
   Record the amixer state: the USBStreamer hw volume must be at its
   0 dB power-on value.

Targets at -20 dBFS: ch 1 and ch 2 at most 0.53 V rms, ch 3 at most
2.0 V rms. Confirmation at -10 dBFS: ch 1/2 at most 1.68 V rms, ch 3
at most 6.3 V rms. If the ratio of the two readings is not 10 dB
+/-1 dB, stop and tell the AE.

Scope handling (safety-critical for the amp):
- Class D amps usually have bridged outputs: neither speaker terminal
  is ground. NEVER attach a probe ground clip to a speaker terminal;
  it is tied to mains earth and can short the amp output.
- Measure differentially: CH1 tip on +, CH2 tip on -, both ground
  clips unconnected, Math = CH1 - CH2; or use a differential probe.
- Both probes on 10x, same V/div, compensated. Never defeat the
  scope's mains earth. 20 MHz bandwidth limit on.
- Read the RMS (cycle RMS if available) of the Math trace with at
  least 10 cycles on screen. Cross-check: Vpp / 2.83 within 10%;
  otherwise add averaging.

After: mark each knob with tape and a pen line and photograph it; any
knob move voids H. Record channel, dBFS, V rms and Vpp in the session
log for the AE. The home-lab venue gains are derived from these
numbers (AC6).

Owner question (AE, via team-lead): whether the oscilloscope has two
channels and a Math (CH1 - CH2) function, or a differential probe.

### Session rules (AE)

- Amps on last and off first. The amp power switches (including the
  headphone/IEM amps) are the emergency stop, and a person stays at
  them for the whole time the amps are on.
- After any gate close, mute or GraphManager restart, reading the
  gains does not prove "muted" yet (safety.md, open list row 14).
  Switch the amps off, including the headphone/IEM amps, with
  headphones/IEMs not worn, and keep them off until PipeWire is
  restarted or the Pi is cold power-cycled. Then verify all 16 gains at
  their boot values per safety.md "Gains at boot" (`pw-cli enum-params`,
  node states logged before and after; a `pw-dump` value is never a
  pass, F-395) and check that the gate is closed (row 14).
- No USB replugging; a fixed USB cable with strain relief, no hub.
- One deployed venue, no profile switching or uploads, no web-UI gain
  edits.
- Before the gate opens and after Mixxx starts, a `pw-link -l` check
  that only convolver-out -> USBStreamer AUX links exist.
