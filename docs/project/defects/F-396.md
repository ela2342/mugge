## F-396: Production must never suspend the filter-chain or the USBStreamer node

**Severity:** P1 (audio-engineer); also a safety item after Gate-2 r7 (audio-engineer)
**Status:** open. The config part is in place; verification on the Pi and the config test guard are open; the WirePlumber-restart case is open (r7). Until fixed, the Gate-2 `--wp-restart` cleanup verdict is "STOP, F-396" (fail-closed)
**Found in:** Gate-2 r6b ruling, section 4 (2026-10-26); Gate-2 r7 sandbox (2026-10-26)
**Affects:** the production filter-chain convolver, the USBStreamer sink; the gains readback (F-395)
**Related:** F-395, US-176, D-070 row 15, US-175, safety.md (USBStreamer transient risk)
**Found by:** audio-engineer (owner), architect (co-owner); joint r6b ruling Addenda 1 E and 2 (2026-10-26)

### Description

A suspend of the USBStreamer sink is a stream stop and restart, which
is the transient risk in safety.md. A suspended filter-chain node also
makes the gains readback blind (F-395).

Architect finding: the production config already sets no-suspend props
(but see the r7 result below: they do not hold across a WirePlumber
restart).
`configs/pipewire/30-filter-chain-convolver.conf` (capture and playback
props), `20-usbstreamer.conf` and `21-usbstreamer-playback.conf` set
`session.suspend-timeout-seconds = 0` and `node.pause-on-idle = false`.
`configs/pipewire/tests/test_filter_chain_config.py` guards only the
convolver props. The config does NOT exclude "never run since boot":
the convolver chain has no `node.always-process` (only
`25-loopback-8ch.conf` sets it).

Gate-2 r7 sandbox (2026-10-26): across a WirePlumber restart every node
went to suspended, the filter-chain, its output node and the USBStreamer
stand-in included, although the selftest filter-chain sets the same
no-suspend props as production. So the props do not protect against a
WirePlumber restart. On the Pi, a USBStreamer suspend is a stream stop,
so F-396 is also a safety item (audio-engineer).

The mechanism is unknown. Production may differ, because GraphManager
links the output to the USBStreamer; the Pi check settles it (architect).

### Acceptance criteria

1. Verified on the Pi: the F-395 step 1 OBSERVE records the states of
   the filter-chain, its output node and the USBStreamer node at idle
   and right after boot.
2. The config test guard is extended to `20-usbstreamer.conf` and
   `21-usbstreamer-playback.conf`.
3. Observed on the Pi: the states of the USBStreamer node, the
   filter-chain and its output node across one WirePlumber restart
   (CHANGE session, announced, nothing connected), and pw-mon over
   step 6 in every Gate-2 --wp-restart run on the Pi.
4. If the USBStreamer node suspends across a WirePlumber restart on the
   Pi, the fix prevents it, or safety.md names the WirePlumber restart
   explicitly as a transient event with F-396 (it already counts it as
   a USBStreamer interruption).
