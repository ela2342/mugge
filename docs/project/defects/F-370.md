## F-370: Pi 5 bootloader hook rewrites config.txt and MAP.txt on a no-op deploy

**Severity:** low (quality-engineer; architect ruling: P2, not a merge blocker, not US-163 scope)
**Status:** open (P2)
**Found in:** `nix/nixos/boards/pi5/bootloader.nix` (hook step 2 and the MAP.txt write)
**Affects:** every Pi 5 deploy; the vfat power-loss window
**Related:** F-369, D-069, US-163 (AC18c, CM-DEP-.89-3 step (c))
**Found by:** quality-engineer (CM-DEP-.89-3, 2026-10-25)

### Description

On a no-op redeploy the hook rewrites `config.txt` and `MAP.txt` with
identical content. AC18c passed on content ("unchanged" means the
content, CM ruling), but each rewrite reopens the accepted vfat
power-loss window (bootloader.nix step 2). The concern is that
window, not flash wear.

### Acceptance criteria

1. The hook skips the write when the new content is identical
   (`cmp -s` before `mv`).
2. A no-op deploy leaves both files untouched (mtime unchanged); the
   pi5-prune-gens check or board-guard covers it.
