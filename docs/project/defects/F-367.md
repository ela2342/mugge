## F-367: greetd crash-loops on the Pi 5 because there is no DRM device

**Severity:** medium
**Status:** guarded in US-163 (`f87c162b`, deployed in `132997f9`); real fix in US-172 (PO ruling, 2026-10-25). The US-163 no-crashloop AC passes at `132997f9`
**Found in:** US-163 T49 on `mugge-pi5-deploy` (`798f2dc7`); greetd/labwc greeter
**Affects:** Pi 5 local display (D-022), Mixxx on HDMI, DJ mode on the Pi 5
**Related:** US-172, US-163 (T49, AC21, no-crashloop AC), D-022, D-069
**Found by:** quality-engineer (US-163 T49, 2026-10-24/25)

### Description

On the first deploy, greetd restarted about every 10 s (about 45
restarts in 8 minutes) without being marked failed. There is no DRM
device: vc4 and v3d are built as modules but not loaded and
`/sys/class/drm` is empty, so the labwc greeter cannot start.

Root cause (architect): the Pi 5 firmware applies no overlay at all,
not only vc4. `config.txt` has both `dtoverlay` lines, the generation
directory has the README and both dtbo files, and `os_prefix` and
`overlay_prefix` point there, yet nothing is merged; every GPU/HDMI DT
node stays `status=disabled`. `/dev/dri` was confirmed absent again on
`nkifli3r` (boot `45ebdf64`). Evidence:
`~/mugge-pi5-prewipe-20261024/diag-F367-overlay*-798f2dc7.txt`.

### Fix in US-163 (guard)

`f87c162b` guards the display stack on this image while no DRM device
exists: greetd is condition-skipped. T49 criterion amended (QE): after
at least 10 minutes of uptime, NRestarts is at most 3 for every
service, and both `systemctl --failed` and `systemctl --user --failed`
are empty. Deploy 2 (`132997f9`): greetd condition-skipped, user@1000
up from boot via linger, T49 PASS (11 min, max NRestarts 0).

### Acceptance criteria

1. US-163 no-crashloop AC: no unit (system or user) is failed or
   restart-looping after boot. Done at `132997f9`.
2. US-172: overlays applied, a DRM device present, greetd/labwc
   running, greetd ordered after the DRM device.
