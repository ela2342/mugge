## F-318: Non-JACK streams may auto-link straight to the USBStreamer since D-065

**Severity:** high, rising to CRITICAL if the Pi test confirms the USBStreamer is the default sink (joint position of audio-engineer and security-specialist, 2026-10-24)
**Status:** open — (a) implemented on `story/US-173-first-dj-session`, needs Pi evidence; (b) not started
**Found in:** `nix/nixos/audio/wireplumber.nix` (D-065, commit `991ff456`), `21-usbstreamer-playback.conf` (priority.session=2000), `deny-usbstreamer-alsa.lua`
**Affects:** D-065, D-043, D-031, D-009, F-292
**Related:** F-298 A6(iii) (no-bypass test), F-334 (local-demo WP policy divergence); **fix story:** US-159 (B1, B2, T6)
**Found by:** security-specialist (retroactive review of `991ff456`, 2026-10-24); sent to audio-engineer as well (veto domain)

### Description

Removing `90-no-auto-link.conf` re-enabled WirePlumber's standard policy.
`node.autoconnect=false` is set only on our own nodes (USBStreamer sink,
convolver) and on JACK clients (80-jack-no-autoconnect). It does not stop
WP from linking other streams (PulseAudio / ALSA-plugin clients) to the
default sink. The default sink is likely the USBStreamer hardware sink
(priority.session=2000; the convolver sets none). `deny-usbstreamer-alsa.lua`
blocks opening the ALSA device, not links.

So the only remaining protection is GM's `link_audit`. It is a single layer,
it acts only after the link exists (some audio gets through first), and
there is none while GM is down or restarting (RestartSec=3). Such a stream
would play with no crossover, no D-031 protection and no gain attenuation.

### Evidence required

On the Pi under a CM session, amps OFF: stop GM, play a stream from a
PulseAudio client, and check with pw-link whether WP linked it directly to
the USBStreamer.

### Candidate fixes (superseded 2026-10-24 by the required fixes below)

Make the convolver the default sink; lower the USBStreamer sink's
priority.session; a pipewire-pulse rule forcing node.autoconnect=false; or
disable only WP's linking hooks instead of all of policy.standard.
D-065 is not accepted until this is resolved.

### Joint position (audio-engineer + security-specialist, 2026-10-24)

- AE and security reached the same conclusion independently. This is the
  AE review of D-065.
- **Clients affected:** any client with default autoconnect, i.e.
  pulse.enable / alsa.enable clients such as a browser, paplay, ALSA
  "default", or desktop sounds.
- **Worst case:** full-scale, unfiltered signal on the 7 W CHN-50P mains.
- **Burst behaviour:** `link_audit` (`link_audit.rs`, all 8 ports,
  event-driven) can let up to about one quantum (≤21 ms) through before it
  destroys the link. WP may then re-link, so the burst can repeat. It does
  nothing while GM is down.
- **Acceptance requires a non-reactive layer;** `link_audit` alone is not
  enough. AE veto applies.
- **Preferred fix:** make the default sink a silent dummy or the convolver
  input, and give the USBStreamer a low priority.session. A pulse-only
  autoconnect rule is not enough, because it misses native PipeWire and
  ALSA clients.
- **Interim rule 2:** play nothing on the Pi except Mixxx, Reaper and the
  signal generator.

### Required fixes and evidence (audio-engineer, architect, 2026-10-24; US-159 B1/B2/T6)

BOTH are required:
- (a) **Prevention:** the USBStreamer is not selectable as a default sink
  (not exposed as a regular Audio/Sink, or blocked via PW permissions;
  stronger than a low priority.session).
- (b) **Enforcement:** GM destroys any non-GM link to USBStreamer playback,
  event-driven on registry link-added, logged, with the gate open or closed.

**Rationale (AE):** a 0 dBFS bypass with bass content on a CHN-50P at
450 W with no crossover exceeds Xmax by about 17 dB, a mechanical hazard
even when brief.

**Evidence test (amps OFF, CM CHANGE session):** pw-play to the default
sink delivers zero frames to the USBStreamer ports, and the time to destroy
a forced direct pw-link is measured and reported.

local-demo cannot reproduce this path today (F-334).

### (a) Prevention: WirePlumber policy (worker-3, 2026-10-25, US-173)

Agreed with the architect, security-specialist, audio-engineer and
quality-engineer. Files: `configs/wireplumber/scripts/mugge-no-bypass.lua`,
`configs/wireplumber/55-mugge-no-bypass.conf`, wired in
`nix/nixos/audio/wireplumber.nix`.

- WirePlumber links no playback stream at all, whatever the target. Only
  subjects whose `media.class` starts with `Stream/Input/` (capture,
  including sink-monitor capture) keep the stock policy. A missing or
  unknown `media.class` is denied. A denied client gets "no target node
  available" from WirePlumber; pw-play and paplay then exit.
- The USBStreamer (by node name, ALSA path and node group) and the
  convolver are never chosen as `default.audio.sink`, even if
  `default.configured.audio.sink` names them.
- Two hooks share the same deny check: one after every target finder and
  before `linking/prepare-link`, and a final guard before
  `linking/link-target`. The final guard logs an error if it ever fires.
- The component is `required`: if the script fails to load, WirePlumber
  does not start.
- Log lines: `F-318: mugge-no-bypass loaded, ...` at start; one warning
  per stream and target, `F-318: denied auto-link of stream node.id=...
  node.name=... application.name=... application.process.binary=...
  application.process.id=... pipewire.sec.pid=... to target node.name=...`.
  The binary and the pids come from the owning client.
  `pipewire.sec.pid` is from the socket credentials; the
  `application.*` values are what the client claims.
- Links that GraphManager makes are not WirePlumber session items and are
  not touched.

**Test:** `nix build -L .#checks.aarch64-linux.wireplumber-no-bypass`
(`nix/checks/wireplumber-no-bypass.nix`). It uses the PipeWire and
WirePlumber packages and the WirePlumber data dirs of
`mugge-pi5-deploy`, so it runs the deployed config:
- Lua unit tests: 19 of 19 pass. Five mutants of the script each fail them.
- Service integration on a real PipeWire 1.6.2 + WirePlumber 0.5.12 stack
  with null-sink stand-ins for the USBStreamer and the convolver: 17 of 17
  pass. Cases: pw-play to the default, to the USBStreamer and to the
  convolver; a `target.object` metadata move; pw-loopback; paplay through
  pipewire-pulse; capture and monitor capture still linked; default sink
  never protected, also with `default.configured.audio.sink` set;
  re-enumeration; a GM-style pw-link survives rescans; WirePlumber and
  PipeWire restarts; hook order; final guard silent.
- Hook order: for every playback stream, the finder lines come first,
  then the deny hook's own line (`F-318: mugge-deny-playback: denied item
  ...`), then WirePlumber's prepare-link line, the one WirePlumber anchor
  (0.5.12). 11 sequences checked. A self-test feeds the checker 7 broken
  orders and it rejects each one, so a lost ordering constraint fails the
  check. With the policy disabled the order case fails too.
- The check writes `summary.txt` (counts, versions, negative control) and
  prints the PipeWire/WirePlumber startup lines and `wpctl status`. It
  fails if `wpctl status` does not list the stand-ins.
- Negative control with the policy disabled: 14 of 17 fail, including
  pw-play linked by WirePlumber and the USBStreamer stand-in as the
  default sink.

**Seen in the test, not caused by F-318:** a WirePlumber restart re-creates
the ports of adapter nodes it adopts, which drops their links. It happens
with the policy disabled too. GraphManager must re-link after a
WirePlumber restart; this test does not check that.

**Still open:** the Pi evidence test above (CM session, amps off) and (b)
GraphManager enforcement. F-318 stays open until both are done.
