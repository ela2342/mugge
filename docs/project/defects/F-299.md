## F-299: D-009 compliance not guaranteed between bins or below 20 Hz (generator and verifier)

**Severity:** medium (D-009 compliance gap; no damage risk today: overshoots are fractions of a dB)
**Status:** open
**Found in:** `src/room-correction/room_correction/verify.py:45-82` (`verify_d009`)
**Affects:** D-009 (cut-only, -0.5 dB margin), D-031 (sub excursion below 20 Hz), F-262
**Found by:** audio-engineer (F-262 review with architect, 2026-10-24)

### Description

`verify_d009` checks |H| only at the filter's native FFT bin centres and
only between 20 Hz and 20 kHz. Overshoot between bins, and any bump below
20 Hz, go undetected. On the subs, a bump below 20 Hz is a speaker-excursion
issue (D-031), not just a headroom one.

### Generator half (folded in per audio-engineer, 2026-10-24)

`combine.py` rebuilds from an already-truncated filter and truncates again
without re-checking D-009 (one-shot re-clip). Together with the verifier gap,
the final output is not guaranteed to be within the D-009 margin. AE ruled
these are two halves of one problem with one joint fix.

### Fix

Resolved by F-262 review conditions 2 and 3: a shared |H| measurement helper,
oversampled at least 4x and covering DC to Nyquist, used by both
`combine.py` and `verify_d009`. AE review condition on the F-262 fix PR.
